# Gold redesign — asset notes

Created for the KRL Teams redesign using the built-in image-generation tool.

## Generated artwork

- `images/gold/bengal-sunset.png`: cinematic homepage landscape illustration.
- `images/gold/integrated-farm.png`: conceptual miniature farm illustration used on the homepage and Farm 360.

These are creative illustrations, not documentary evidence of any real holding. Their on-page captions make that distinction.

### Hero prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-width cinematic website hero background for Krishi Ratna League Bengal, a warm inviting premium farmers' league website with BKS Pujo's midnight, burgundy, antique gold and champagne theme. Create a beautiful ultra-wide 3:2 editorial photographic illustration of a Bengal rice landscape at golden sunset. Low aerial perspective over meandering geometric flooded paddy plots and a village pond, rice stalks at bottom right, distant palms and faint village silhouettes, amber sunlight reflecting off water and atmospheric golden mist. Rich warm espresso shadows, bronze and golden ochre highlights, hints of burgundy sunset, natural luminous image, aspirational and alive, realistic rural Bengal landscape, sophisticated travel-film still, textured film grain. Main scenic detail right half, dark visually quiet left half for large ivory text added in HTML. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI. This is illustrative landscape artwork, not documentary evidence of a named farm.

### Farm concept prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium editorial artwork for an integrated-farming website feature card. Landscape 3:2 composition, a beautifully crafted three-dimensional miniature Bengal integrated farm, isometric view, small rectangular pond reflecting golden evening light, carefully planted vegetable beds, rice paddies, narrow irrigation channels, a small earthy rural farm hut and banana plants, miniature farm on a subtly bevelled block of dark rich soil, no people. Photorealistic tactile model materials, tasteful and refined museum exhibition model, grounded agricultural detail. Studio backdrop deep burgundy #710912 fading to midnight #0D0D0D, warm espresso #362822 shadows, antique gold #C28D39 rim lighting and champagne #EFE5D2 accents. Inviting and luminous, subtle fine suspended gold particles, polished premium visual, generous margin around the miniature, crisp tiny details. No labels, no words, no logos, no watermark. Clearly a conceptual model of a farm, not a real named farm.

## Vector assets

All fifteen team identities use the original official PNG logos in `images/teams/`, including homepage cards, the team register, detail pages, table identities and farm profiles. The header, footer and favicon use the original `images/krl-logo.png`. The earlier `images/gold/<team-id>.svg` and `krl-emblem.svg` presentation experiments are no longer used.

`images/gold/icons.svg` is the shared vector icon sprite. The existing West Bengal outline is recoloured in antique gold without altering its geometry.

## Visual reference

[BKS Pujo live reference](https://bks-pujo-demo.vercel.app/) and [source repository](https://github.com/aryansingh173173-lab/bks-pujo-demo). Exact primary colours: #0D0D0D, #710912, #362822, #EFE5D2 and #C28D39. The active typography uses the repository's locally hosted Outfit family throughout English content, with Noto Sans Bengali for Bengali. Newsreader and IBM Plex files from the earlier design pass remain on disk with their licences, but the final typography stylesheet overrides those families.
